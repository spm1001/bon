## For Claudes to come

The session converged on a substrate decision worth carrying into understanding.md:

- **Semantics-wrapped-round-a-database is the asset; the DB is commodity.** bon is already semantics-over-Dolt (twice over — the atom *plus* Dolt's git-semantics: branch/diff/history). So the shared work-atom should be **owned by bon/Dolt**, not rented from Todoist; faces (Todoist, kuwivo, CC CLI) are **projections that specialise**, not rival stores.
- **The dual-memory-model.** Todoist drops the outcome→action link and stores *context* (the human's hard bit is where/when/headspace, not *why*); bon stores link + spec and no context (the agent's hard bit is the link it can't retain across cold starts; it needs no reminder-context because being handed the task *is* the reminder). Photographic negatives — complementary, not rival.
- **THE GIFT (proven in the spike):** Todoist's goal-link is **orthogonal to the project**. An action lives in a context bucket *and* points at its outcome at once — the GTD "link-not-stored" weirdness is solved *without de-contexting the action*. This is what makes a clean human face possible at all.
- **Todoist's structural holes (confirmed from the `Doist/todoist-ai` MCP source):** no definition-of-done field, no dependency primitive. A spec or dependency in Todoist is therefore unenforced + synthetic → the atom must be enforced in bon, which can refuse a bad one.
- **The atom design:** one atom, two tiers — `reminder` (loose, human, title + context) and `spec` (agent-grade, enforced why/what/done + parent). Discipline applied **on promotion, not on everything** — bon's mandatory-brief-on-all was correct when bon was agent-only and becomes a misfeature the instant a human shares the store. Full sketch + test plan: `docs/2026-06-17-atom-sketch.md`.
