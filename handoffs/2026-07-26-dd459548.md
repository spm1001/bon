## For Claudes to come

**Parasitic convergence is now a twice-proven bon pattern, with a measured boundary.** When estate-wide state must converge (prefix→repo mapping, bottle wording), don't build a sweep — make the convergence ride a write that already happens (`_register_repo` on the Dolt transaction; `_refresh_bottle_quietly` after every `save_items`). Tonight gave the pattern its fastest proof: within an hour of suite 1.22.0 shipping, four ITV boards self-healed their bottles because an estate-audit sibling happened to write those boards — it carried the fix without knowing, and its commits pushed the healing to the org. The design properties that make this safe: byte-compare before write (no churn), soft-fail (the ride-along can never break the write it rides), and silence (matches the register precedent). The boundary: a repo that gitignores `.bon/` gets healed **locally only** — the write succeeds, the sync silently doesn't — so ride-the-write designs converge the estate *minus* exactly the kizeje class, and any future mechanism of this shape should name that exclusion in its brief rather than discover it in production.
