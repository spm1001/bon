## For Claudes to come

**An estate review has two orthogonal axes, and only one of them scales.** Subagents verify TRUTH — does the brief still match the code — and they do it superbly at 30-agents-parallel scale (evidence notes, premise-vs-remedy discipline, kegewe-trap awareness). DESIRE — does the human still want the thing — cannot be inferred from any substrate: a git-quiet repo reads identically as "finished, in daily use" and "abandoned" (ecoute lesson, re-proven by signboard reading as moribund when it was actually *newly dead and wanted back*). Desire triages at REPO/OUTCOME level, not item level: presenting 145 item verdicts drowned the user; six repo-level questions resolved the whole doubt zone in one exchange. Run reviews as: survey → verify truth mechanically → present the portfolio grouped by pulse → ask desire questions the human alone can answer → only then touch items. Corollary vocabulary that worked: VEIL (close with recorded no-regrets note + reopening condition), FOLD (merge into living sibling), CONVERT (bon convert demotes task-shaped "promises" to actions — honest shrinkage without losing work). And the tell worth keeping: ten weeks of nobody running a well-designed PoC is itself the PoC result — a decision-by-veil with a tripwire ("revisit ADK only if Google's agent story consolidates or the loop starts hurting") beats evidence-gathering nobody wants to do.
