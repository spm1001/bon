The session-start briefing's "Suggested" section pulls from the previous handoff's Next items, not from `bon list --ready`. This is a deliberate design choice: the outgoing Claude curates what the incoming Claude should work on. The full ready list is available on demand via `bon list --ready` or the bon.txt disk file, but the briefing stream carries only the baton pass. Bon IDs in the Suggested section are preserved naturally — they appear when the outgoing Claude referenced them in the handoff's Next section, giving the incoming Claude a direct `bon work` handle.
