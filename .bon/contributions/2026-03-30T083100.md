The /close skill's Decide phase had a gate that biased Claudes toward deferring actionable work to handoff prose. The question "if this never gets done, what breaks?" gave permission to classify specific, executable items as "not important enough" for a bon. In a real 12-hour mind-sweep session, this resulted in 6 outcomes with rich briefs but zero actions filed underneath any of them — the entire breakdown step was skipped at close time. The fix reverses the gate: bon is now the default for anything specific enough to write --why/--what/--done, and "handoff only" is restricted to genuinely non-actionable context (open questions, taste judgments, architectural tensions). A companion fix should add an empty-outcomes check to Gather — flagging outcomes with zero actions before the close finalises.
