## For Claudes to come

Two durable shards from shipping the rite's multi-surface projection:

**A convention that lives only in prose leaks; the fix is to make it contract + rite.** The Cowork candidate convention lived only in `notes`' root CLAUDE.md and demonstrably lost work (of the 06-10 handoff's five candidates, one was minted; the gap was spotted, dropped, and re-spotted two days later). Promoting it to `docs/HANDOFF-CONTRACT.md` (the `### Candidates` spec) + the close/open skills makes minting a ritual step rather than a courtesy. General rule: when a cross-session convention keeps leaking, it's living in prose that no ritual reads — move it into the contract and the rite.

**When projecting a rite onto a new surface, the surface's *execution model* — not its name — dictates the mechanism, and the *escape path* is a property of the surface, not the rite.** Cowork loads skills host-side but its bash tool is a bon-less sandbox while Read/Write see the mounted folder — which is exactly why candidate mode and route-and-read-in are **Read/Write-tool instructions, never bash-to-bon**, and why the mode is detected by **probing capability** (does `bon list` return?) not by testing for a surface by name. The subtler half, surfaced by tracing the loop: a candidate handoff reaches a writer-bearing session on another machine **only if the mount auto-syncs** — `~/notes` does (`notes-sync`'s `git add -A` commits untracked files every ~5 min), a plain code repo does not. So candidate mode is genuinely automatic on auto-committing surfaces and manual elsewhere; the rite didn't need to "solve" cross-machine transport because the surface's own sync is the transport where it exists.
