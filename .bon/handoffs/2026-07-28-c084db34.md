## For Claudes to come

**understanding.md is two documents wearing one name, and the test that separates them is: could a capable reader re-derive this from the artefacts in front of them?** If yes it's a **cache** — it costs tokens on every read, it drifts, and when it drifts it doesn't go quiet, it lies with confidence. If no it's a **record** — it cost an incident, it's recoverable from nothing else, and it appreciates. The discriminating question is not "is this about code" but **"did we pay for this, or is it just true?"** — which is why "The landmines" is *pure record* despite reading like architecture (nobody discovers that `bon wait` silently destroys tactical state by reading `cli.py` politely), while "`cli.py` is the bulk of the codebase — every command loads, mutates, writes back" is a cache that any session re-derives in ninety seconds.

This matters beyond one file, because it's the general form of a question this estate keeps asking under different names: **which parts of the apparatus are about the world, and which are about the model?** Session boundaries, rejected alternatives, and incidents are world-facing — no capability jump retires them, because the process really does die and code really does record only what was built. Cached architecture, prescriptive step decomposition, and verify-your-work nags are model-facing, and Braadbaart's test (in `~/scratch/compass_artifact_*.md`) says those *expire on upgrade*. The practical upshot for anyone writing into understanding.md at close time: **before adding a paragraph, ask which kind it is.** Records earn their place permanently. Caches are a loan against future drift, and the interest is paid by whoever next trusts them.

One guard against over-applying this, learned by nearly over-applying it tonight: the "delete 80% of your scaffolding" advice that motivates the test was measured on *coding evals*, comes with credible dissent, and is explicitly inverted in this partnership's own `CLAUDE.md` (which turns off the vendor's conciseness and scope-limiting guidance). The cache/record cut survives scrutiny on its own merits. The 80% number does not travel.
