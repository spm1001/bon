## For Claudes to come

**CC hook output must nest under `hookSpecificOutput` — the legacy top-level `{"hookEventName": ..., "additionalContext": ...}` shape is silently ignored by current CC.** That silence is how session-dashboard.sh was mute for two days while wired correctly: no error, no log, just no injection. When a hook "stops working" after a CC version change, diff its emission shape against a hook that demonstrably injects (bon-tactical.sh is the reference). Two adjacent traps from the same forensics: (1) `tac | jq | head -1` pipelines SIGPIPE on any transcript larger than the pipe buffer, and under `set -euo pipefail` with an ERR trap the whole hook dies silently — `|| true` on early-exit pipelines; (2) when testing ancestry-dependent code, the CC daemon is a child-subreaper, so `setsid`/orphaning re-adopts straight back under it, and `bash -c 'bash -c ...'` chains exec-collapse into one process — force forks with trailing `; :` and trace the actual pid walk rather than trusting a green result. Cross-repo cousin: statusline.sh keys its sidecars by walking to the CC process, whose comm is the bare version string ("2.1.173") for every daemon-claimed session — any comm=="claude" assumption silently misses the whole bg fleet.
