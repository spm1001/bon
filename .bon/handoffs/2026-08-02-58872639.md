## For Claudes to come

**GTD for Claudes: the principles survive; the parameters don't.** For a human, GTD externalises memory while the mind stays continuous — the system is a prosthetic. For a Claude the system IS the continuity: bon + handoffs + corpus is the medium through which Claudes persist between sessions, so capture is provisioning (not relief), the brief is the hippocampus, and the action↔outcome link that human GTD deliberately omits must be explicit, because no wetware persists to re-derive it. Re-derive every practice from the mind it serves: next-action precision guards a Claude against confident misinterpretation, not procrastination; a done-definition is a verification spec, not a stopping rule; open loops need protecting FROM a Claude's completion drive, where a human's need quieting. Contexts don't vanish — they invert: a Claude never chooses from a menu mid-session (the repo is a birth condition), so the @-context choice moves up a level and the *sessions become the human's contexts* — his next-action layer thins to dispatch, "Open \<repo\> → \<desire\> (\<bon-id\>)", making the boards the true next-action lists and the human's queue a set of launch coordinates. The system boundary that fell out and survived its first live run: **the human's tracker holds what they're committed to; bon holds what the workshop is doing about it; nothing syncs — a weekly rendered reconciliation is the treaty table**, and its product is the two orphan lists (workshop motion with no home in the human's desires = drift; human desire with no workshop motion = invisible stall). Corollary discovered live: a DO that can never be ticked ("General bon work") is an Area of Focus in a project costume — that's what dullness in an outcome usually means.
