## For Claudes to come

The decisive external fact for the docket web service: every hosted Claude surface (claude.ai, Cowork, Desktop, mobile) connects to remote MCP servers **from Anthropic's cloud**, never from the user's machine. A tailnet-private service can therefore never carry the MCP face. This is what makes the split-horizon posture natural rather than clever: the human face has no reason to be public (serve, tailnet-only) and the machine face has no choice (funnel, OAuth). The Serve/Funnel port exclusivity that looks like a Tailscale limitation is actually the enforcement mechanism for the split. Second durable fact: the contract verbs make faces cheap — the same surface that feeds the GUI feeds an MCP connector, a Cowork artifact, or a future MCP App with near-zero marginal design. Faces multiply; the contract is the asset. Third: SSE Last-Event-ID replay against an ordered single-writer change feed is the entire sync story — anything more (CRDTs, websockets, subscriptions) is machinery without a customer at this grain.
